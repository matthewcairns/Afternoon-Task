## Connect your Outlook (Microsoft 365) calendar so meetings record automatically

1. Click your initials (top right) and open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Integrations → Calendar** and click **Connect Microsoft**.
3. Sign in with your work Microsoft 365 account and accept the permissions. Your calendar stays private to you.
4. Under **Recording preferences**, choose which meetings the bot should join (for example, all external meetings with a Teams, Zoom or Google Meet link).
5. For each meeting, admit the **Afternoon meeting bot** from the lobby when it asks to join. If it isn't admitted, nothing is recorded, and a missed recording cannot be recovered.
6. After the meeting, the transcript appears on the client's **Meetings** tab with speakers labelled and timed.

Troubleshooting: if the bot doesn't appear, check the meeting has an online meeting link and that the calendar connection still shows **Connected** under Integrations.
